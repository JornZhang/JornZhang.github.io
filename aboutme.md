I am currently a Postdoctoral Associate in the Department of Biostatistics at the Yale School of Public Health, working with Professor [Hongyu Zhao](https://zhaocenter.org/). I received my Ph.D. in Statistics from [Colorado State University](https://statistics.colostate.edu/) in 2025, where I was advised by Professor [Wen Zhou](https://wp.nyu.edu/rczw/) and co-advised by Professor [Haonan Wang](https://www.stat.colostate.edu/~wanghn/). Prior to CSU, I received a B.A. in Insurance from the [School of Finance](https://finance.nankai.edu.cn/) and an M.A.S. in Applied Statistics from the [School of Statistics and Data Science](https://stat.nankai.edu.cn/) at Nankai University in 2016 and 2018, respectively.


### Research interests:
* Statistical inference under complex dependence and random design 
* High-dimensional statistics and structured regularization  
* Causal inference and high-dimensional mediation analysis
* Statistical learning with external knowledge and semantic embeddings
* Applications to biomedical and other complex data
